# Code of Conduct

## English

### Our Pledge

We as members of the RemaGraph community pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

### Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Enforcement

Anyone who violates this Code of Conduct may be temporarily or permanently banned from participating in this project.

### Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

For questions, please contact a project maintainer.

## 繁體中文

### 我們的承諾

身為 RemaGraph 社群的一份子，我們致力於讓每個人都能在一個友善、包容且不受騷擾的環境中參與本專案。

### 我們的標準

有助於營造正向環境的行為包括：

- 使用友善且包容的語言
- 尊重不同的觀點與經驗
- 樂於接受建設性批評
- 以社群整體利益為優先考量
- 對其他社群成員展現同理心

### 不可接受的行為

不可接受的行為包括：

- 使用帶性意味的語言或圖像，以及不受歡迎的性關注或追求
- 帶有挑釁、侮辱或貶損性質的言論，以及人身或政治攻擊
- 公開或私下的騷擾行為
- 未經同意公開他人的私人資訊
- 其他在專業場合中可能被合理認定為不當的行為

### 執行方式

違反本行為準則者，可能會被暫時或永久禁止參與本專案。

### 出處聲明

本行為準則改編自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1 版。

如有任何疑問，請聯絡專案維護者。
